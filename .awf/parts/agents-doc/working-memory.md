For an external checkpoint with no resident effort, establish and surface its identity and any uncertain interpretation, normalize its leading `Effort:`, `Route:`, `Phase:`, `Workflow:`, and nonempty `Next:` lines, then invoke `awf_adopt_effort` alone with the matching explicit fields.
