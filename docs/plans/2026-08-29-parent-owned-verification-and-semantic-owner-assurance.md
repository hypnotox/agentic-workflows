# Plan: Parent-Owned Verification and Semantic-Owner Assurance

## Goal

Make the parent the exclusive owner of implementation transactions and terminal verification, let it safely reuse structured focused evidence from commit-disabled children, and split separable implementation and assurance work by semantic owner when repeated findings expose an oversized boundary. Host-wide verification serialization, external-prerequisite preflights, serialized integration windows, numeric decomposition thresholds, and expansion into unrelated blockers remain out of scope.

## Architecture summary

The Pi implementation profile retains selected-checkout validation, sequential dispatch, and exclusive parent-batch execution while removing commit mode selection. It snapshots the canonical checkout and fails closed unless it can prove that HEAD is unchanged across the child run. The rendered implementer has one commit-disabled authority and returns a completed or stopped receipt carrying bounded scope, checkout and tree identities, focused command evidence, changed paths, remaining work, deviations, blockers, and generated-output or fixture evidence. The parent independently inventories the real checkout, rejects boundary violations or an unverifiable HEAD, and reuses focused evidence only while its checkout, authority, and overlapping-path predicates stay fresh; staging, staged checks, commits, fast gates, and exhaustive verification remain parent-owned.

Execution and assurance routes identify semantic owners before assigning a broad unit. Independently verifiable owners stay separate, while a cross-owner composition that is itself the protected transaction stays together. Repeated findings of one underlying concern or violated contract across separable owners trigger a finite repartition of remaining work without changing finding classification or opening another verify loop for the originating transaction. Fresh units retain focused proof, and terminal assurance covers composition and the complete range. Existing unrelated-blocker routing remains separate from the active outcome. Independent cheap operations may be grouped in one parent route when they are safe and share context, without creating a new transaction owner or weakening path confinement.

## Definition of done

- `dod: parent-owned-transaction` Every implementation child is commit-disabled and gate-free; the parent alone integrates, stages, checks staged state, commits, runs the fast gate, and runs exhaustive terminal verification, while the Pi runtime fails unless it proves the selected HEAD stayed unchanged.
- `dod: fresh-focused-evidence` Every child returns a closed structured receipt whose checkout, scope, tree state, commands, results, deviations, blockers, and generated evidence can be independently validated and selectively reused or invalidated by the parent without replacing a gate.
- `dod: semantic-owner-units` Broad implementation and assurance work is grouped by semantic ownership, and repeated same-concern findings across separable owners trigger a finite nonnumeric repartition without recursively reopening the originating review.
- `dod: bounded-composition-assurance` Fresh owner units retain focused proof and ordinary bounded review, terminal assurance covers cross-owner composition and the complete range, unrelated blockers stay outside the active outcome, and independent cheap operations may be batched only without transferring authority or weakening confinement.
