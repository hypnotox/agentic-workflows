# Plan: State ownership pattern and its two conversions

## Goal

Implement ADR-0180: establish `code-design/state-ownership` as a pathless global topic with four
reasoned claims and one test-backed claim, and convert its two named consumers, the three cached
derivations on `internal/project.Project` and the Pi subagent extension's preference store. Non-goals:
this plan does not split `internal/project`, does not touch the `workingCurrentState` snapshot corpus
path, and does not widen `currentState.testGlobs`.

The design and its rationale live in ADR-0180. This plan is the execution record only.

## Architecture summary

Four phases, each one independently green transaction.

Phase 1 lands authority: the topic metadata and part carrying the four `Backing: unbacked` claims, the
`code-design` commit-scope widening with its `update` operation on ADR-0178's classification claim, the
reviewer and workflow-chain pointers, and the glossary term. It appends the ADR's `Implementing` event
and its first `Applied` batch.

Phase 2 converts the Pi preference store: `resolveChildModel` takes an already-derived
`EffectivePreferenceState` instead of the store, and the store's closure-mutable slots become one load
step returning an immutable value.

Phase 3 converts `internal/project`: `Corpus` and `Topics` become operation-local derivations threaded
as parameters, `effSkills` becomes a threaded value rather than a field, `beginInvocation` is deleted,
and the coverage exclusions that cited the shared cache are re-derived.

Phase 4 proves it: one structural test asserting no production function in `internal/project` writes a
`*Project` field outside the function that constructs that value, the `project-derived-state-ownership`
claim with its proof marker, the ADR's second `Applied` batch, and the ADR and plan status flips.

The batch split is forced by two mechanisms: `internal/topic/corpus.go` requires a claim's Origin ADR to
carry an applied `add` for that exact claim id, and `internal/topic/markers.go` requires a `Backing: test`
claim to have a proof marker at all times. So claim 5's part text, its proof test, and the second Applied
line must land in one commit.

  ### `invariant: single-derivation-producer`

  Within one operation, a new or deliberately converted derived value is produced exactly once and every consumer receives it rather than re-deriving it; the rule counts productions per value per operation, not producers per type.
  Origin: ADR-0180
  Backing: unbacked
  Verify: For each changed derived value, enumerate its production sites within one operation and confirm exactly one, with every other consumer receiving the value.
